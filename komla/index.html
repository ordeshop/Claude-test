About

I photograph the moments people actually remember.

I'm KOMLA — a photographer based in Vienna, working across weddings, editorial and brands. My approach is quiet and observational: I'd rather catch the real laugh than stage a perfect one.

Ten years behind the camera have taken me from wedding fields to studio sets and product tables. Whatever the brief, the aim is the same — honest images with a point of view, shot mostly in natural light.
