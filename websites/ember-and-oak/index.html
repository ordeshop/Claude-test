The fire is lit at dawn and never goes out through service.

No dials, no gas — just white oak and fruitwood, and heat learned by hand. Every plate carries a little of that smoke.

The menu is short on purpose. It changes with the season, so it's honest about what's good this week and nothing else. Come hungry; leave slowly.

— Marta & Jonah, at the hearth
