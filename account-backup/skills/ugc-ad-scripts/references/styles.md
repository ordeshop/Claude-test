# Style guide — faceless sub-styles + talking-head personas

## Faceless sub-style 1: Satisfying product demo ("Amazon finds")

The aesthetic gadget/home/beauty demo. Faceless, hands showing one product do its one clever thing.

- **Feel**: clean, bright, aspirational-but-attainable, a little wholesome. "Look at this cool thing."
- **Shots**: macro close-ups of hands operating the product; a slow reveal of the "aha" feature;
  before/after of the little problem it solves. Often ends on "watch till the end for [feature]."
- **Text**: light narration in overlays — "For those who…", "Watch till the end ✨", "Mini but powerful".
- **Sound**: gentle ASMR of the product (clicks, whirs, snaps) OR a soft trending audio. Upbeat, calm.
- **CTA**: "it's on my Amazon / linked below / storefront in bio."
- **Best for**: gadgets, kitchen/home organization, desk tools, beauty devices, novelty gifts.

## Faceless sub-style 2: ASMR restock / refill

The deeply satisfying restock. Faceless hands refilling, organizing, decanting, unboxing in quantity.

- **Feel**: relaxing, hypnotic, loop-friendly, "clean with me" / "restock with me."
- **Shots**: pouring product into clear containers; lining items up; peeling film; wiping a surface;
  the shelf/fridge/vanity going from empty to full. Long smooth takes, minimal cuts.
- **Text**: minimal — a title card ("restocking the snack drawer 🫧") and maybe a soft CTA. Often wordless.
- **Sound**: the star. Crinkle, pour, tap, click, zip, seal, liquid glug. Captured ASMR, not music.
- **CTA**: soft — "restock with me," "everything linked."
- **Best for**: consumables, drinks/snacks, cleaning supplies, containers, anything bought in bulk or refilled.

You can **blend**: a restock that ends by demoing the hero product's feature, or a demo that opens with a
short satisfying restock beat.

## Talking-head personas

When the format is talking-head (creator or AI avatar), a believable persona sells it:

- Match the persona to the buyer: a tired late-30s guy for a snoring fix; a skincare-curious woman in
  her 20s for a beauty app; a freelancer at a messy desk for a finance tool.
- Setting should be a real room with lived-in detail (not a studio). Name the light (window/lamp).
- Keep wardrobe/hair/setting identical across shots so the avatar stays consistent when stitched.
- Voice is casual and first-person: "I tried this and honestly…". Never corporate.

## Universal realism rules (both formats)

- ≤5s shots; reuse the reference frame; muted natural color.
- One named soft light source + direction per shot.
- One imperfection per shot, kept aesthetic (a crumb, a wrinkle, a droplet, a bit of clutter).
- Handheld drift / macro phone feel; never static-perfect.
- Attach the matching negative prompt every shot; for faceless, watch hands and label text especially.
