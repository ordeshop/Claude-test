# UGC Ad Script — GlowLog (AI skincare app)
Platform: TikTok · Length: ~28s · Angle: "I finally know if my routine is actually working" · Creator persona: woman, mid-20s, skincare-curious, films in her bathroom
Assumptions: leading benefit = tracking progress with AI photo analysis; buyer = 20–30s women frustrated by spending on products with no visible payoff. Redirect me if the angle should be acne, anti-aging, or budget instead.

## Creator & setting — Kling reference image
Reference-image prompt: A 25-year-old woman with light freckles and no makeup, hair in a claw clip, wearing an oversized grey tee, standing in a small bathroom with warm morning light from a window on camera-left; toothbrush cup and a couple of skincare bottles cluttered on the counter, phone-selfie framing, muted natural tones, slight film grain.
Negative prompt: plastic skin, waxy skin, oversaturated, overly smooth, digital sharpness, cgi look, airbrushed, perfect symmetry, warped hands, extra fingers, floating objects, stiff motion, studio lighting

## Hook options (test all three)
A. "I wasted $300 on skincare before I tried this." — formula: pattern interrupt · visual: holds up phone with a progress photo grid
B. "Nobody tells you if your skincare is actually working." — formula: curiosity gap · visual: confused glance at two nearly identical selfies
C. "POV: you finally stopped guessing if your routine works." — formula: POV/contrarian · visual: relieved half-smile to camera

## Shot-by-shot
### Shot 1 — Hook · 0:00–0:04 · talking-head
- Spoken: "I wasted three hundred dollars on skincare before this."
- On-screen caption: "$300 wasted 😬"
- Kling motion prompt: Young woman leans toward phone-selfie camera in a bathroom, soft window light from camera-left, gives a quick embarrassed laugh and glances away then back, slight handheld drift, muted natural tones.
- Realism cues: handheld drift; embarrassed laugh + glance; cluttered counter behind her

### Shot 2 — Problem · 0:04–0:10 · talking-head
- Spoken: "I could never tell if anything was actually doing something."
- On-screen caption: "same face for months??"
- Kling motion prompt: Same woman shrugs and tilts head, holds two similar selfies up next to her face, soft window light camera-left, she fidgets with the phone, handheld drift, wrinkled tee, muted tones.
- Realism cues: handheld drift; shrug + fidget; wrinkled shirt

### Shot 3 — Solution/Demo · 0:10–0:18 · B-roll (screen record)
- Spoken: "So now I just snap a photo every morning and the app tracks it."
- On-screen caption: "one photo a day"
- Kling motion prompt: Close over-the-shoulder shot of the phone screen as a thumb taps a camera button and a progress grid loads; slight hand tremor, warm bathroom light, muted tones. (Production note: screen-record the real app here instead of generating, for an accurate UI.)
- Realism cues: hand tremor; real UI; natural light spill

### Shot 4 — Proof · 0:18–0:24 · talking-head
- Spoken: "Four weeks in and the difference is actually right there."
- On-screen caption: "week 1 → week 4"
- Kling motion prompt: Woman looks genuinely surprised at the phone, eyebrows up, small "oh" mouth, soft window light camera-left, leans in, handheld drift, muted natural tones.
- Realism cues: handheld drift; genuine surprise micro-expression; messy background

### Shot 5 — CTA · 0:24–0:28 · talking-head
- Spoken: "It's free to start — link's right below, go check it."
- On-screen caption: "link below 👇 free to start"
- Kling motion prompt: Woman points down toward the caption area, easy smile, quick shrug, soft window light camera-left, slight handheld drift, muted tones.
- Realism cues: handheld drift; point + shrug; natural light

## Example 2 — FACELESS (ASMR restock / satisfying demo)

Product given by user: just "Fizzé — reusable glass drink bottles + flavor pods you refill at home."

---

# UGC Ad Script — Fizzé (refillable flavor-pod drink system)
Platform: TikTok · Format: Faceless (ASMR restock + demo blend) · Length: ~24s · Angle: "the most satisfying way to quit buying soda"
Assumptions: hero moment = the pod clicking in and the water fizzing/coloring; buyer = 20–35s aesthetic-kitchen types trying to cut soda/energy drinks. Redirect me if you'd rather lead on cost savings, sugar-free, or eco/less-plastic.

## Setting & hands — Kling reference image
Reference-image prompt: Macro close-up of two clean hands with short natural nails on a light oak kitchen counter by a window, a row of empty glass bottles, a small tray of colorful flavor pods, a water carafe; soft daylight from camera-left, a few water droplets on the counter and one bottle slightly out of line, phone-macro framing, muted natural tones, slight film grain.
Negative prompt: warped hands, extra fingers, fused fingers, mangled fingers, plastic skin, oversaturated, overly smooth, digital sharpness, cgi look, floating objects, morphing product, warped text on packaging, stiff motion, studio lighting

## Hook options (test all three)
A. Text: "watch the water change color 🫧" — approach: reveal-first · Visual: open on the fizz blooming color through clear water
B. Text: "POV: you finally quit soda" — approach: POV · Visual: hand lining up empty bottles in soft light
C. Text: "the last step is so satisfying" — approach: curiosity · Visual: a pod hovering over a bottle, about to click in

## Shot-by-shot
### Shot 1 — Hook · 0:00–0:04
- On-screen text: "watch the water change color 🫧"
- Kling motion prompt: Extreme macro of clear water in a glass bottle as a burst of color blooms and fine bubbles rise, soft daylight from camera-left, gentle handheld drift, a single water droplet sliding down the glass, muted natural tones.
- Sound / ASMR: soft fizz and rising bubbles, a faint glass tap
- Realism cues: macro drift; real condensation droplet; soft window light, not glossy

### Shot 2 — Setup/"before" · 0:04–0:09
- On-screen text: "no more cans piling up"
- Kling motion prompt: Macro of two hands lining up three empty glass bottles on an oak counter and sliding a tray of colorful pods into frame, soft daylight camera-left, a bottle nudged straight, slight handheld drift, a crumb near the tray edge, muted tones.
- Sound / ASMR: glass clinking softly on wood, the light rattle of pods in the tray
- Realism cues: macro drift; simple two-hand action; crumb + slightly-off bottle imperfection

### Shot 3 — Demo/Restock · 0:09–0:15
- On-screen text: "pop a pod in"
- Kling motion prompt: Macro of a thumb pressing a flavor pod into a bottle's cap until it clicks, then twisting the cap on, soft daylight camera-left, a tiny fumble as the pod seats, gentle handheld drift, muted natural tones. (Small held object — consider filming the real pod insert for an accurate click.)
- Sound / ASMR: a crisp click, then the squeak-twist of the cap sealing
- Realism cues: macro drift; single simple press; slight real fumble; no lingering on pod label text

### Shot 4 — Payoff/Reveal · 0:15–0:20
- On-screen text: "one press = fizzy, flavored, done"
- Kling motion prompt: Macro as the sealed bottle is given a slow tilt and the liquid fizzes and fully colors, condensation forming on the glass, soft daylight camera-left, slight handheld drift, muted tones.
- Sound / ASMR: fizz swelling, liquid swirl, a satisfying settle
- Realism cues: macro drift; real condensation; soft natural light across the glass

### Shot 5 — CTA · 0:20–0:24
- On-screen text: "everything's linked 👇 restock with me"
- Kling motion prompt: Macro of a hand setting the finished bottle into a row of matching colored bottles, then sliding the tray of pods back into place, soft daylight camera-left, gentle handheld drift, one droplet on the counter, muted tones.
- Sound / ASMR: soft glass line-up clinks, tray slide
- Realism cues: macro drift; simple placing action; droplet imperfection

## Sound design summary
Pure captured ASMR, no music: lead with fizz + bubbles, then glass clinks, the pod click, and the cap twist. Keep it close-mic'd and crisp; these sounds are the retention mechanic. If a trending audio is preferred instead, drop the ASMR and cut the actions to the beat — don't layer both.

## Text overlay script (all captions in order)
watch the water change color 🫧 → no more cans piling up → pop a pod in → one press = fizzy, flavored, done → everything's linked 👇 restock with me

## Optional voiceover (only if needed)
Not needed for this one — the visuals and sound carry it. (If you want one soft line, use it on Shot 4: "one press and it's done.")

## TikTok caption + hashtags
quit soda and honestly do it for the fizz sound alone 🫧 #restock #asmr #satisfying #sodalover #kitchenfinds

## Production notes
- Generate each shot as a ≤5s Kling clip; reuse the reference frame so hands/counter/bottles stay consistent.
- Shot 3 (small pod in fingers) is the highest warp risk — film the real pod-click insert if you can, or generate with the negative prompt and pick the cleanest take.
- Avoid lingering close on the pod/bottle labels; Kling warps readable text.
- Keep all sound as close-mic'd ASMR; don't mix with a trending track.
